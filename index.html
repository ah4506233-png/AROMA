Alopecia (Hair Loss)

Ranges from mild shedding to complete baldness.
Symptoms: Gradual thinning or Patchy hair loss (coin-sized spots).

Causes & Types

  1. Genetics: Family history (Androgenetic).
  2. Autoimmune: Alopecia areata (immune system attacks follicles).
  3. Physical Stress: Surgery, illness, shock (Telogen effluvium).

Herbal Treatments

1. Saw Palmetto (Serenoa repens)

Mechanism: Combats androgenetic alopecia by blocking 5-α reductase, reducing scalp DHT levels.

Active Compounds: Fatty acids, Phytosterols (β-sitosterol).

2. Ginseng (Panax ginseng)

Mechanism: Increases blood flow to the head.

3. Rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis)

Mechanism: 12-methoxycarnosic acid inhibits 5-alpha reductase; 1,8-cineole improves circulation.

Marketed Products

  • TOLA® lotion: Ginseng, Garlic, Rosemary, Jojoba.
  • ALOEKITA DS® shampoo: Caffeine, Saw palmetto, Aloe, Tea tree.
